# Face Tracking

With the FT Extension installed, you can layer emotional nuance from a stick and gesture-based eye symbols and effects on top of your real facial movements. For installation, see [Installing the FT Extension](/3plus1/getting-started/ft-install).

---

### Mouth During Speech

During normal speech, VRChat's standard lip sync takes priority. While mouth tracking is active, holding **Point** with either hand prioritizes the FT mouth even while speaking. Releasing it restores normal priority. This works even with Modifier off.

## Stick Directions

The direction selects the nuance and the tilt distance sets the strength. The center is neutral.

<DirectionPad
  up={{icon:'CONFUSE', label:'Up: CONFUSE', desc:'Confusion'}}
  left={{icon:'ANGER', label:'Left: ANGER', desc:'Angry'}}
  center={{label:'Center', desc:'Neutral'}}
  right={{icon:'PLEASURE', label:'Right: PLEASURE', desc:'Fun / happy'}}
  down={{icon:'SORROW', label:'Down: SORROW', desc:'Sad'}}
/>

The stick alone changes eyebrow nuance. Eye symbols are added with the gestures under "Effects with Modifier ON" below. Tilt clearly in the intended direction, not diagonally.

:::warning[Resetting the stick]

Move the stick's cursor back to the center. Closing the menu may not reset it.

:::

## Effects with Modifier ON

**Fist** is the fist gesture recognized by VRChat; either hand works. The expression changes depending on whether one hand or both hands make a fist. A fist at neutral does not show a symbol.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---------|---------------|
| FT does not work at all | Check, in order: VRCFaceTracking reception, the module, VRChat OSC, AUTO |
| Only symbols do not appear | Check that Modifier is ON and VRChat recognizes the gesture |
| Hearts or stars do not appear | Check nuance restrictions and return the stick to center |
| Highlight/pupil size remains after releasing watery eyes | Blink once with both eyes |
| Mouth differs only while speaking | Standard lip sync has priority; use Point for the FT mouth |
| Capacity error | Check the final parameter cost including all add-ons ([Parameter Capacity](/3plus1/ft-extension/)) |
